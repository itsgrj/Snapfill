# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- A new **Ignored** tab in the toolbar popup lists every site Snapfill stays away from.
- Add a site by typing it in, even one you are not on. Tick the box to cover all its
  subdomains too, so `mybank.com` also covers `netbanking.mybank.com`.
- Remove a site from the list with one click, and Snapfill fills there again.
- The **Fill on this site** switch now adds to and removes from the same list.

## 1.0.1

- The "Remembered" message now shows the Snapfill icon instead of a plain symbol.

## 1.0.0

The first public release.

- Fills every field it recognises on your first click in a form, and moves your cursor to the
  first field still empty.
- Work, Personal and Dummy profiles. Each site remembers the one you used there.
- Learns fields you type by hand and fills them on later forms, even when they are labelled
  differently.
- Undo for a fill, and for anything it learned.
- Pause any site from the toolbar popup.
- Alt+Shift+F fills the form you are on.
- Never fills passwords, card numbers, one time codes or government ID numbers, and leaves
  sign-in boxes to your password manager.
