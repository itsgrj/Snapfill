# Privacy

Snapfill fills forms with details you give it. Those details are personal, so here is exactly
what happens to them.

## What Snapfill keeps

- The details you type into its popup: name, date of birth, address, email, phone, company and
  job title, for each profile.
- Fields it learns from forms you fill by hand, with the site you typed them on and the date.
- Which profile each site uses, and which sites you paused.

## Where it keeps them

In your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`), on your computer. Nothing is
synced across devices and nothing is sent anywhere. Removing the extension removes all of it.

## What Snapfill never does

- It never sends anything over the network. There is no server, no analytics and no tracking.
- It never fills or learns passwords, card numbers, one time codes, PAN, Aadhaar or passport
  numbers.
- It never fills a form until you click one of its fields.
- It never reads pages beyond the form fields you are filling.

## The Dummy profile

Dummy makes up a name, email and phone each time it fills. Emails use the reserved
`example.com` domain, so they can never reach a real person. Nothing typed while Dummy is in use
is saved.
